Compose a post

This page doesn't publish anything by itself — it's just a form that formats a post correctly for you. Fill it out, hit Generate, copy the code, and paste it as a new entry near the top of the posts array in thoughts.html. Commit and push, and it's live.

Deleting a post instead?

No tool needed — open thoughts.html, find that post's { ... } block in the posts array, and delete the whole thing (including its trailing comma). If it was the last post in the array, make sure the block before it doesn't end in a comma either. Commit and push — it's gone from the live site.
