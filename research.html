$30 trillion in motion.

The next great wealth transfer isn't about houses. It's about where $30 trillion in concentrated equities moves — and what platforms win by being accessible first.1

Sep 2026 — Educational Analysis

Where wealth sits today

Of the roughly 5.7 billion adults globally, only 1.5% hold net assets above $1 million. That tiny sliver — 57.5 million people — controls most of the world's wealth.1

The age-wealth gap (1983–2022)

Here's what happened: older Americans got richer relative to everyone else. People 75+ went from having about average wealth (1.05×) to having 1.55× the average. People under 35 went the opposite direction — from 0.21× down to 0.16×.2

The transfer problem

What's leaving: $30 trillion in stocks, mostly owned debt-free by older Americans

Where it's going: To younger Americans who own houses and debt, not stocks

What happens next: Young inheritors will sell concentrated stocks and buy different things — alternative markets, crypto, etc.

The stock market is dangerously concentrated

The top 10 companies in the S&P 500 used to be about 20% of the index. Now they're 41%. This matters because when billions in inherited stock hits this market, it can move prices hard.3

The capital flows next

① Equities will move around (but not all stay in stocks)

$30T in inherited stock needs to go somewhere. Capital gains tax will push heirs to diversify. Some stays in stocks, some goes elsewhere.1

↓

② Alternative markets (Kalshi, prediction markets)

Younger people have never used a traditional broker. They'll use Kalshi or similar platforms because it's one click, not three phone calls. The winners capture massive AUM here.

↓

③ Crypto gets a real shot (if regulations don't kill it)

Bitcoin's 6.21% monthly returns will attract some capital. The volatility scares many, but a generation raised on apps will experiment here.4

↓

④ Housing loses out

Young inheritors are already house-poor. They're not buying second homes. Liquid, tradeable assets win. Real estate gets less capital.

How to win the transfer

🎯 Make it simple

One click to start. No forms. No waiting. That's Kalshi.

📊 Fast execution

Inheritors move money in weeks, not months. Real-time settlement matters.

🔗 Meet them where they are

API first. App first. Traditional advisor model loses.

The money on the table

Even if you capture just 2% of the wealth that gets reallocated = $600 billion+ in new AUM over a decade. Traditional brokers can't compete on speed or simplicity. Platforms that are easy win.

The squeeze on real estate

Inheritors won't buy vacation homes. They'll put money into markets where they can trade fast, see results immediately, and move the capital when they want. Real estate becomes a drag on returns for this cohort.
